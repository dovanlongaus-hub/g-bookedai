# Lesson 1: What is AI?

## Module: AI Foundations (Track A)

### Learning Objectives
- Understand what AI is and what it is not
- Learn the difference between AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning
- Discover how AI is being used in real businesses today
- Identify 3 areas where AI can help your work

### Lesson Outline

#### 1. Introduction (5 min)
- Welcome and overview
- What we will cover today

#### 2. What is Artificial Intelligence? (10 min)
- Definition: AI is software that can learn from data and make decisions
- Brief history: From rule-based systems to modern generative AI
- Key concepts: Training data, models, inference, prompts

#### 3. AI vs ML vs Deep Learning (5 min)
- AI: Broad field of intelligent systems
- ML: AI that learns from data (no explicit programming)
- Deep Learning: ML using neural networks
- Generative AI: Creates new content (text, images, code)

#### 4. AI in Business Today (10 min)
- Customer service: Chatbots and virtual assistants
- Marketing: Content generation and personalisation
- Operations: Process automation and forecasting
- Finance: Fraud detection and risk assessment
- Education: Personalised learning and tutoring

#### 5. Hands-on: Your First AI Interaction (15 min)
- Open Google Gemini or ChatGPT
- Write your first prompt
- Compare results from different prompts
- Learn the "good prompt" formula: Context + Task + Format + Constraints

#### 6. Exercise
- Write 3 prompts for your specific work context
- Compare outputs from Gemini vs ChatGPT
- Document what worked and what did not
